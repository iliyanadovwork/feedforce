A TikTok pulled in via link (see [reposting from a link](/blog/repost-tiktok-to-instagram-from-link)) can land in three different places on Instagram, and picking on autopilot — always Reels, say — leaves reach on the table one way or another. Each destination optimizes for something different.

## Reels: discovery, for your strongest clips

Reels get genuine algorithmic distribution to people who don't already follow you — this is the destination built for reach beyond your existing audience, and it's also the most public, longest-lived placement of the three. Reserve it for clips you'd stand behind being shown to a stranger with zero context: your strongest hooks, your most complete ideas, the ones that don't depend on already knowing your account.

## Feed: durability, for things worth finding later

A feed post (including a repurposed clip presented as a still-image or carousel rather than autoplay video — see [turning a video into a carousel](/blog/turn-video-into-carousel)) sits on your profile grid indefinitely and gets found by people browsing your account's history, not just people scrolling a live feed. This is the right destination for a clip that's more reference than moment — something someone might search your profile for later, not just catch once.

## Story: immediacy, for lower-stakes or time-bound content

Stories are seen mainly by people who already follow you, disappear in 24 hours, and reward immediacy over polish — the lowest-stakes destination of the three. This is where a clip you're not fully sure clears the bar for Reels belongs, or where a genuinely time-bound repost (something tied to a moment that won't matter tomorrow) makes more sense than a permanent placement.

## The combination that usually works best

Rather than picking one destination per clip, the strongest pattern is often layered: your best clips go to Reels for discovery, get a Story mention pointing back at the Reel (not duplicating it — a teaser, a reaction, an arrow), and occasionally also anchor a feed post if the content has lasting reference value. Weaker or more time-bound clips skip Reels' bigger stage and go straight to Story, where a lower bar and a forgiving, disappearing format fit better.

## Automating the destination choice

If you're [automating your cross-posting](/blog/automate-content-using-brand-template) rather than doing it by hand each time, an [If node](/blog/if-node-branching-recipes) checking a simple signal — engagement on the source TikTok, a manual "strength" tag you set, or a keyword suggesting time-sensitivity — can route a repost toward the right destination automatically, rather than defaulting every single repost to the same placement regardless of what actually suits it.
